## Preserved boundaries and remaining architecture

Canonical DIDAR sessions remain a separately authenticated surface. Native ACL/deactivation is not represented as global canonical account revocation. Supplier business eligibility and existing merchant grants continue to constrain canonical operations independently. The generic Vendor payments route remains denied because the shipped `seller_payment` query alias is unavailable; DIDAR financial reports retain their original ledger authority.

This review does not approve native-only commerce as a competing stock/order/finance authority, merge identities or claim durable two-phase native Product projection reconciliation. Cross-supplier retailer economics, broader authority/session convergence and staging host execution require their respective architecture/release approvals. Browser visual acceptance, historical-clone audits, clean first-Admin install and full regression are separate workstream evidence, not executions claimed by this reviewer.
