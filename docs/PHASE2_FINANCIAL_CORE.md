# هسته مالی دیدار — محیط آزمایشی

Gate 1 implementation: additive `finance-schema.sql`, immutable contract requests/reviews, checkout terms, supplier events, `/didar/finance`, Persian `/manage/finance`. No backfill of historical orders. Verified sandbox payment records one exposure per reserved UID (one aggregated line for explicitly legacy stock); intake alone creates none. Ownership is a contractual classification; it never proves a physical delivery or title transfer. DIDAR_OWNED records zero external entitlement. Buy-to-resell commercial recognition is provisional at verified sale in this sandbox, not a legal purchase-debt policy.

Gold is integer micrograms **fine**, not grams at 750. Actual mg × purity (per thousand) produces fine µg. Making is added independently of distribution/retail/VAT. Percent making rounds up once per unit to µg; split gold share rounds down; the remainder converts at immutable checkout IRR per 750g-equivalent **gram** with IRR ceiling. At 65,000,000 IRR/g750: 100g/750 +12% → 84,000,000µg fine =112g750 entitlement; 50% split →42,000,000µg +3,640,000,000 IRR. Retail 3%/7% and margin VAT10% produce 8,073,000,000 IRR, independently. Legacy fixed-IRR making stays IRR, explicitly in the snapshot; it is never invented as gold.

Contracts default to the documented sandbox GOLD_ONLY/consignment terms (version 0) until a requested contract has admin approval and its effective time has arrived. Terms freeze at checkout; later approval/backdating does not rewrite accepted orders. Due dates start from verified payment. Contract requests and reviews are append-only. Supplier requests are own-supplier only; review/refund is admin-only. Financial writes require local sandbox runtime. Statements redact customer payment/pricing and customer identifiers.

Refund is a **synthetic full-unit financial reversal**, separately invoked after physical return receipt for itemized goods. A physical return alone does not assert a bank refund. Each sale can be reversed once; no journal/event deletion or physical UID reset. Legacy whole-line refunds require explicit admin evidence. Real payment, legal recognition, statutory VAT, title and wallet licensing require professional review before any production use.

Verification status is tracked per gate in PROGRESS/DELIVERY; Mac and physical RFID remain pending. No live Tahesab endpoints or credentials are used.

## Gate 2 — تدارک، خزانه و تسویه دستی

`finance-procurement-schema.sql` adds one immutable purchase request per positive-gold sale event (including existing financial events, never old unrelated orders). `/didar/treasury` and `/manage/treasury` derive state from the same append-only financial events. Requests are not executed trades. Admin approves requests and separately records and approves explicitly synthetic/manual-booked purchases with source, document, actual mg/purity, acquisition IRR cost, immutable rate, custody and title evidence.

Global transaction lock serializes financial commands; database-unique command keys and JSONB payload comparisons reject changed-payload retries. Allocations require approved requests/purchases and confirmed purchase title. Cumulative allocations cannot exceed either source purchased gold or original non-refunded entitlement. One purchase can serve many requests and one request can use many purchases. Allocation alone does not change the obligation or establish delivery. Separate title-transfer and physical delivery/held-for-supplier evidence are required before any partial gold discharge. IRR discharge requires synthetic payment evidence. Outstanding gold **and** IRR must be zero before `settled` appears. Due/overdue and owned/unallocated purchase stock are derived from source facts.

Unexecuted allocations and unallocated purchases can be reversed without deleting history. Title/custody facts block ordinary allocation cancellation. Settlement reversals require explicit QA return/recovery evidence; the original receipt/title history remains and is not silently undone. Full financial refunds after physical return can produce a negative supplier statement (recoverable advance if already settled); they do not assert that external funds/gold were recovered. No automated external trade/bank action exists. Supplier reads contain own requests/allocations/settlements, with purchase desk/cost documents restricted to admin.

## Gate 3 — دفتر دوطرفه و کیف پول

`finance-ledger-schema.sql` and `finance-ledger.ts` project the **same** immutable financial events into one journal/event and append-only debit/credit postings. IRR, fine-gold µg and custody-only memo µg balance independently; PostgreSQL deferred constraints reject empty/unbalanced journals and wallet overdrafts. Posting insertion also takes the financial transaction lock. Repeated migration/projection skips already projected events; old unrelated paid orders are never reconstructed. `/didar/ledger` and Persian `/manage/ledger` show the chart/balances/correlations. Supplier reads exclude internal sales/cash/revenue and competitor accounts.

The sandbox chart separates clearing, VAT, agent versus principal revenue, supplier gold liability/IRR valuation, rial liability, COGS, owned jewelry, melted available/allocated gold, title clearing, valuation differences and consignment memo custody. Purchase and allocation use recorded synthetic costs; title and discharge are separate. No statutory correctness is inferred: account names/mappings, recognition, VAT and principal-agent treatment are **SANDBOX_PROVISIONAL / PROFESSIONAL_APPROVAL_REQUIRED**.

New posted intake creates aggregate source-backed custody events, never purchase debt. Old intake is not fabricated. An explicitly documented **owned** contract and accepted unsold physical batch can receive a QA opening cost via `jewelry_value`; cost splits over actual item fine weights with final residual assigned deterministically. Sale snapshots freeze that cost and UID; COGS reverses on synthetic financial refund. No documented owned cost means valuation remains explicitly pending, never an invented historical purchase cost. Physical return memo movements are separate from refund journals, preventing double restocking of the memo ledger. The physical UID registry remains the only inventory source.

`/didar/wallet`, Persian `/account/wallet`: customer/supplier gold and rial pending, available, reserved, pledged balances derived from postings. Only admin may record documentary synthetic funding/release; owners may reserve/release/pledge/transfer available funds internally. No real withdrawal operation exists. Concurrent spending serializes; overdrafts are rejected in service and database. Payable-to-wallet reduces the **same** supplier liability, crediting its wallet, including correlated gold valuation; it never duplicates debt. Gold wallet transfers carry recorded valuation proportionally. Real wallet licensing/custody and money movement remain disabled pending legal approval.

## Gate 4 — ته‌حساب، فقط خروجی و شبیه‌ساز مستقل

**Concrete Tahesab endpoint mapping: BLOCKED_BY_SPEC.** `api-1.json` is absent from this checkout and available attachments. No endpoint, vendor serialization, account-sign semantics, import behavior or proprietary schema has been invented. `concreteTahesabRequest()` deliberately fails closed. Supply the private OpenAPI contract before implementing/claiming vendor compatibility; no sensitive spec/credential should be committed publicly.

Implemented `didar-aggregate-v1`, not a claimed Tahesab contract: immutable versioned merchant/invoice/account mappings, source-claimed accounting closes, append-only outbox attempts and persistent PostgreSQL mock documents. `/didar/tahesab` + Persian `/manage/tahesab` prepare dry-run exports and local mock acknowledgements/retries/reconciliation. `Factor_Code` is deterministic and 41 characters; each merchant/event is claimed once across overlapping periods. Cutoffs are [start,end), completed times only, grouped by Tehran Jalali day, merchant, event type, fine basis/actual purity, instrument and account; debit/credit are retained separately rather than netting different movements. Explicit memo projections distinguish actual recorded procurement assigned to a merchant, allocation, physical handover, incoming goods and reversals. Actual unassigned global purchases remain in Didar treasury, not fictitiously assigned to a merchant. Export figures come from journal postings, including independently balanced memo instruments.

Payloads omit buyers, addresses, UID/EPC, order/reservation/event IDs and purchase counterparties. Internal source manifests retain correlations. Supplier UI exposes its own mapping, status and merchant statement, with full internal journal export restricted to admin. The local mock detects stable intent, transient/rejected responses and externally edited/deleted mock documents without silently overwriting them. Real provider operations, imports and online writes remain disabled.

`bun run tahesab:mock` optionally starts a separate provider-independent mock on **127.0.0.1:9109**; only its own synthetic Bearer value `DIDAR_LOCAL_MOCK_ONLY`, `DBname` beginning `QA_`, and `[Factor_Code,1,aggregate]` mixed-type envelope are accepted. These mock endpoints/envelopes **are not Tahesab endpoints**. Its synthetic documents persist in ignored `.local/`; the integrated application mock persists in PostgreSQL. Nightly Medusa job prepares dry-run closes for the previous completed Tehran calendar day at UTC midnight in local sandbox only; it does not dispatch anything. Current supported local dates are 2026+ (Iran UTC+03:30 without DST). Native Mac acceptance remains pending.
