# هسته مالی دیدار — محیط آزمایشی

Gate 1 implementation: additive `finance-schema.sql`, immutable contract requests/reviews, checkout terms, supplier events, `/didar/finance`, Persian `/manage/finance`. No backfill of historical orders. Verified sandbox payment records one exposure per reserved UID (one aggregated line for explicitly legacy stock); intake alone creates none. Ownership is a contractual classification; it never proves a physical delivery or title transfer. DIDAR_OWNED records zero external entitlement. Buy-to-resell commercial recognition is provisional at verified sale in this sandbox, not a legal purchase-debt policy.

Gold is integer micrograms **fine**, not grams at 750. Actual mg × purity (per thousand) produces fine µg. Making is added independently of distribution/retail/VAT. Percent making rounds up once per unit to µg; split gold share rounds down; the remainder converts at immutable checkout IRR per 750g-equivalent **gram** with IRR ceiling. At 65,000,000 IRR/g750: 100g/750 +12% → 84,000,000µg fine =112g750 entitlement; 50% split →42,000,000µg +3,640,000,000 IRR. Retail 3%/7% and margin VAT10% produce 8,073,000,000 IRR, independently. Legacy fixed-IRR making stays IRR, explicitly in the snapshot; it is never invented as gold.

Contracts default to the documented sandbox GOLD_ONLY/consignment terms (version 0) until a requested contract has admin approval and its effective time has arrived. Terms freeze at checkout; later approval/backdating does not rewrite accepted orders. Due dates start from verified payment. Contract requests and reviews are append-only. Supplier requests are own-supplier only; review/refund is admin-only. Financial writes require local sandbox runtime. Statements redact customer payment/pricing and customer identifiers.

Refund is a **synthetic full-unit financial reversal**, separately invoked after physical return receipt for itemized goods. A physical return alone does not assert a bank refund. Each sale can be reversed once; no journal/event deletion or physical UID reset. Legacy whole-line refunds require explicit admin evidence. Real payment, legal recognition, statutory VAT, title and wallet licensing require professional review before any production use.

Verification status is tracked per gate in PROGRESS/DELIVERY; Mac and physical RFID remain pending. No live Tahesab endpoints or credentials are used.

## Gate 2 — تدارک، خزانه و تسویه دستی

`finance-procurement-schema.sql` adds one immutable purchase request per positive-gold sale event (including existing financial events, never old unrelated orders). `/didar/treasury` and `/manage/treasury` derive state from the same append-only financial events. Requests are not executed trades. Admin approves requests and separately records and approves explicitly synthetic/manual-booked purchases with source, document, actual mg/purity, acquisition IRR cost, immutable rate, custody and title evidence.

Global transaction lock serializes financial commands; database-unique command keys and JSONB payload comparisons reject changed-payload retries. Allocations require approved requests/purchases and confirmed purchase title. Cumulative allocations cannot exceed either source purchased gold or original non-refunded entitlement. One purchase can serve many requests and one request can use many purchases. Allocation alone does not change the obligation or establish delivery. Separate title-transfer and physical delivery/held-for-supplier evidence are required before any partial gold discharge. IRR discharge requires synthetic payment evidence. Outstanding gold **and** IRR must be zero before `settled` appears. Due/overdue and owned/unallocated purchase stock are derived from source facts.

Unexecuted allocations and unallocated purchases can be reversed without deleting history. Title/custody facts block ordinary allocation cancellation. Settlement reversals require explicit QA return/recovery evidence; the original receipt/title history remains and is not silently undone. Full financial refunds after physical return can produce a negative supplier statement (recoverable advance if already settled); they do not assert that external funds/gold were recovered. No automated external trade/bank action exists. Supplier reads contain own requests/allocations/settlements, with purchase desk/cost documents restricted to admin.
